About Me

Turning data into intelligence, intelligence into impact

I'm an AI Engineer who ships. At Cognizant I support Sprinklr as a third-party engineer: debugging REST APIs with Postman, digging through logs, integrating AI systems, and resolving 500+ complex production cases across backend and product workflows, working directly with clients.

My research roots run deep. As an international AI research intern at Yuan Ze University in Taiwan, I trained neural networks on 2M+ simulated circuit scenarios, automating HSPICE data generation with Python and deploying a Streamlit app that gives circuit designers real-time predictions. I'm also a co-author of the BinBot paper presented at ICICIS 2025.

I build in public. My GitHub holds a hybrid GraphRAG retrieval engine, Docent (an AI curator for research papers), and ops-copilot (a multi-agent operations copilot). Toolbox: Python, LLMs and RAG, PyTorch, SQL, Oracle Cloud, MLOps, Git. I'm open to full-time AI engineer roles. If you're building with GenAI, let's talk.

GenAI, RAG, Knowledge Graphs

GraphRAG Hybrid Retrieval Engine

Self-corrective retrieval over messy docs: Neo4j knowledge graph plus Qdrant dense and sparse vectors, fused with RRF, reranked by a cross-encoder, and checked by a LangGraph CRAG loop. Benchmarked on 16 multi-hop questions: context precision 0.88 vs 0.62 for vector-only.
